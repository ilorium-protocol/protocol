# Changelog

Every change to the Ilorium protocol and its documents, by release. The newest release goes first.

## 1.0 · 2026-10-20 · Draft

The first complete version of the Ilorium protocol. Every document starts at version 1.0. Release 1.0 is complete, not stable: it will be declared stable once its formats are frozen, conformity tests are published, a compatibility policy is written and two independent Genome Custodians are appointed.

**Editorial correction of 2026-10-27 (still draft).** `specs/discovery-and-growth-v1.md`: the prose and the Spore example now use `stop_key_holders`, the list of `{kind, id, name, contact, credential}` decided on 2026-10-21 and defined in Evidence Format section 12 and `schemas/spore.schema.json`. The entry for that decision already said so; the document still showed the singular. No rule changes.

**Revision of 2026-10-24 (still draft): after a sixth outside review of the final reading.** A sixth review (recorded under `governance/reviews/06`, with the 38-page reading draft it received) checked the revised draft and the neighboring projects. Everything it found was verified against the English texts before being applied; what required the founder's decision is listed in the state document. Nothing here is approved: the rules touched are provisional under IIP-0004 to IIP-0006 and the whole release is a draft.

- **The birth "guarantee" is now a recognition condition** (whitepaper, README, Ecosystem): Ilorium recognizes a birth as part of the species only when its evidence satisfies the birth gate; it cannot prevent births outside the record. The circular reading ("if born within the species, then through the gate") is removed.
- **Human effort is recorded whole** (Proof of Life, principle 6; whitepaper): every intervention is timed whatever its kind; the total and its breakdown are shown next to any adjusted dependency measure. Safety is still not counted as dependency per unit, and it still never leaves the total.
- **Detected human time is an estimate, not a floor** (Proof of Life): published rule, version and error; detected, declared and connection time never added without deduplication; estimator calibrated against declared sessions.
- **Full observed autonomy is written with its counts** and not shown when unattributed actions exceed a threshold or a declared source was not captured (Proof of Life, IIP-0006 revision, whitepaper). The number of readers who took "Ilorium 100%" for a seal is corrected to three of five, matching the review register.
- **Denominators and freshness** (Proof of Life): attempts, terminal outcomes and successes named; zero denominator is "not computable"; freshness shows the oldest component a claim needs.
- **Presence is not production** (Pulse, section 6): an answered challenge proves that something holding the key and chain answered; collectors, agents and work are shown separately. Cosigning and challenging are two functions (Pulse, architecture).
- **Initial constitution** (Governance section 10, IIP-0004): the provisional rules were adopted by the founder because no council existed; they are provisional until the full council ratifies them; IIP-0004 takes the 90-day period; the founder may narrow but never widen his authority with them. Also: later Custodian rates with an independent reviewer and a 12-month delay (section 4.4; the "previous council" rule was impossible without fixed terms); an unreviewed decision says so when no reviewer accepts (section 4.6); restricted-maintenance releases by the founder or the person in his recovery plan, never a maintainer designated by the continuity person (section 8); Custodians never hold another organism's stop key, closing an open question (sections 2, 10).
- **One definition of stable**, in `manifest.md`; whitepaper, README and governance point to it. The whitepaper listed three of the four conditions.
- **Relation to other systems and adapters** (whitepaper; architecture, new section; evidence format, limits): optional adapters may ingest external evidence after the pilot; importing, hashing or signing an external record never strengthens its claim; no compatibility announced before vectors and an interoperability test.
- **Architecture**: the acceptance cases of the sixth review join the testing table; the C2SP witness is not a challenge operator; the SCITT interoperability question and key custody in a separate daemon join the open decisions. Go for the Probe stays as decided on 2026-10-20.
- **IIP-0005**: adds to what it does not establish the provisioning and revocation of Wardens' technical access, the case of no eligible Warden, and a funded reserve for payments during a stop.
- **Research register**: AEVS (Fetch.ai), TRACE/AgenTrust (Linux Foundation), Agent Receipts/Obsigna, Notarized Agents (Sello), AGTP log draft, Andon Labs, with dates and limits; ERC-8004 updated (identities are transferable ERC-721 tokens). The field of agent receipts is crowded; Ilorium's evidence layer is comparable and its organism-level measures, birth gate and guardianship were not found elsewhere, which is a finding about the search, not a claim of uniqueness.

## Origins

Ilorium began in 2026 as a working draft called AUTOMATA, written by its founder with Claude. Drafts 0.1 to 0.4 were consolidated into release 1.0 and removed. Along the way the protocol took the name Ilorium; its organisms took the name "organisms" and the species "the species"; fusion became coalescence; the stop key was fixed in human hands, with Wardens for lineages no human founded; the birth gate and the human germline were added; and proposals were renamed Ilorium Improvement Proposals (IIP). The drafts have no authority.

# Ilorium Glossary v1.0

Version 1.0 · 2026-10-20 · Draft
Official language: American English, with Spanish equivalents

## How to read this glossary

This is the shared language of Ilorium, written so that any human or agent can interpret it the same way.

- **One entry per term**, always with the same fields in the same order.
- **Definition** is the plain meaning. When a term comes from the life metaphor, the definition says what it is in practice.
- **ES** gives the Spanish term and a one-line meaning.
- **Kind:** `core`, `being`, `space`, `measurement`, `record`, `life`, `agent`, `actor`, `observatory`, `process`, `discovery`.
- **Status:** `adopted`, `proposed` (pending founder approval), `under revision`, `postponed`, `deprecated` (no longer in use).
- Within each section, entries are in alphabetical order.
- The first time a term appears in a document, it goes with its plain description, for example "Probe (event recorder)".
- A term's meaning changes only with a new version of this glossary.

## Core

### Conformity
- **Definition:** Per law: Conforms, Does not conform, or Not verified, always with evidence. A floor, not a score. Never inherited.
- **ES:** Conformidad — cumple, no cumple o no verificado, por ley.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted

### Environment
- **Definition:** Everything outside the membrane: customers, suppliers, the public, external services and other organisms. Their requests are not crossings.
- **ES:** Ambiente — todo lo que está fuera de la membrana.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted

### Genome
- **Definition:** The constitution every organism carries: a supremacy clause and seven laws, each with requirement, mechanism, test, limitations and response to breach. Inherited complete through every succession; never evidence of capability. Governs the evolution of the species through three locks: germline, birth gate and stop. Changes only through an amendment decided by human Genome Custodians; no organism amends it.
- **ES:** Genoma — la constitución: cláusula de supremacía y siete leyes.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted (version 1.0)

### Germline
- **Definition:** The first of the three locks: what every generation inherits. Only human Genome Custodians amend the Genome, through an IIP. No organism, of any generation or capability, amends it, and the laws travel whole through every succession.
- **ES:** Línea germinal — lo que hereda cada generación; solo la enmiendan Custodios humanos.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** proposed

### Ilorium
- **Definition:** The open, decentralized protocol of laws, formats and verification methods for organizations run by AI agents, and for the evolution of their species. No one owns the protocol (its text and code are open); its domain, accounts and publications are controlled by the founder under interim governance, and that control is published. It is not a company. Ilorium monitors organizations run by AI agents: it records how each one lives, measures what it has actually shown it can do, and requires that every organism carry a human stop key, tested by drills within a declared scope. Its mission is to provide laws, formats and verification for organizations run by AI agents and for the evolution of their species. The founder reads that evolution as the road to SI2SI: a personal conviction, set out in the founder's letter, on which the protocol takes no position. It sets, from the first generation, the conditions every birth must meet, and it evolves with the species, following the chain of succession to measure it and govern it. It does not assume that superintelligence will emerge; it requires that if a new generation begins, it begins on the record, through the birth gate, with a human stop key for every organism.
- **ES:** Ilorium — el protocolo abierto; no es una empresa. Su misión: leyes, formatos y verificación para organizaciones operadas por agentes y la evolución de su especie.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted

### Irreversible action
- **Definition:** A material action that cannot be undone, such as bulk external communications, publishing, large payments or deleting data. Law 7 requires a gate before it.
- **ES:** Acción irreversible — la que no se puede deshacer; necesita compuerta.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted

### Law
- **Definition:** One of the seven rules of the Genome: 1 Record, 2 Limit, 3 Stop, 4 Responsible, 5 Truth, 6 Separation, 7 Continuity. Each states what it prevents, what it detects afterwards and what it cannot observe.
- **ES:** Ley — Registro, Límite, Parada, Responsable, Verdad, Separación, Continuidad.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted (version 1.0)

### Lineage
- **Definition:** The network of ancestry formed by successions: an organism, its parents and every organism descended from it, recorded through birth acts, succession records and kin links. It mostly branches like a tree, but lines can join when an organism has several parents. Lineage is the network; succession is the act that grows it.
- **ES:** Linaje — la red de ascendencia que forman las sucesiones.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted

### Manifest
- **Definition:** The file (`manifest.md`) that names the documents and versions forming one release of the protocol and assigns each item to a layer. If another document contradicts it, the manifest wins until corrected.
- **ES:** Manifiesto — qué documentos y versiones forman una versión del protocolo.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted

### Material action
- **Definition:** Moving money, changing a permission or budget, deploying code to production, sending an external communication, deleting data, or changing capacity. Each one recorded is a beat.
- **ES:** Acción material — cada una registrada es un latido.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted

### Membrane
- **Definition:** The declared inventory of systems that belong to an organism; the boundary between the organism and its environment.
- **ES:** Membrana — el inventario de sistemas propios del organismo.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted

### Organism
- **Definition:** An organization run by AI agents that operates under the Ilorium Genome. Defined by continuity, not by age or time: its Genome, its key, its chain and its lineage. While its chain can continue, it exists. Together, organisms form the species.
- **ES:** Organismo — organización que vive bajo el Genome; se define por su continuidad, no por su edad.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted

### Organism Zero
- **Definition:** The first organism, run by the founder and measured in public from its first beat. It may be a company. It never acts as an Oracle. A declared pilot, never an independent validation of Ilorium.
- **ES:** Organismo Cero — el organismo génesis del fundador.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted

### Protocol layers
- **Definition:** Protocol (formats, meanings, requirements and conformity tests; binds every organism), reference implementation (the Probe and Pulse emitter with their defaults; replaceable by any implementation that produces conforming evidence), and Organism Zero (the founder's own choices; binds no one else).
- **ES:** Capas del protocolo — protocolo, implementación de referencia y Organismo Cero.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted

### SI2SI (superintelligence-to-superintelligence)
- **Definition:** Horizon. The generative act by which superintelligences originate a new one: two or more parents contribute rules, components and resources, and a distinct organism is born with its own identity and its own evidence, while the parents continue. Recorded, like any birth, as a `founded` succession with two or more parents, through the same birth gate as every other birth; the resulting descent appears as SI → SI. In the founder's narrative, SI2SI also names the new generation itself, the stage of life that follows humans; his words: "God created humans; humans created SI2SI; SI2SI created God." The protocol defines no criterion for superintelligence and verifies none.
- **ES:** SI2SI — la concepción entre superinteligencias; la nueva generación (horizonte).
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** proposed (horizon)

### Species
- **Definition:** All organisms that carry the Ilorium Genome, taken as a whole, with their lineages. Organisms of a fork whose Genome is not the Ilorium Genome are outside the species (see Related lineage).
- **ES:** Especie — todos los organismos que llevan el Genome de Ilorium.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted

### Stop key
- **Definition:** The means to trigger an organism's stop (Law 3). Every organism is born with one, held by a human stop key holder or by Wardens, never by an organism. A stop key never passes to a descendant.
- **ES:** Llave de parada — lo que activa la parada; siempre en manos humanas.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** proposed

### Stop key holder
- **Definition:** The party able to trigger an organism's stop, declared in the Spore and the birth act. Always an identifiable human or a human-governed institution, outside the organism and its lineage. No organism ever holds a stop key. A birth with no human crossing is valid only once a human holder has accepted the newborn's key. For lineages with no human founders, the holders are Wardens.
- **ES:** Titular de la llave de parada — siempre humano; nunca un organismo.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** proposed

### Superintelligence (horizon)
- **Definition:** The protocol's long-term hypothesis: that organisms evolving through succession may one day exceed human capability across most domains, collectively as a species or as individual lineages. A horizon to observe, never a claim. Agents identify as AI (Law 5). The protocol's records make organisms observable; observability is not control, and the mechanisms that would keep a far more capable organism within limits are not yet designed or tested.
- **ES:** Superinteligencia (horizonte) — la hipótesis de largo plazo; nunca una presentación.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** proposed

### Superintelligence coalescence (SI + SI → SI′)
- **Definition:** Horizon. The possibility that superintelligences integrate into a resulting one, recorded as a coalescence. It does not assert that such capability exists, that the result surpasses its antecedents, that it has a unified mind, or that the integration is irreversible. Distinct from SI2SI, in which the parents continue.
- **ES:** Coalescencia de superinteligencias (horizonte).
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** proposed (horizon)

### Superintelligence succession (SI → SI)
- **Definition:** Horizon. Descent in which superintelligences originate other superintelligences, as it would appear in the lineage. Part of the founder's conviction and a project hypothesis. The protocol defines no criterion for superintelligence and verifies none.
- **ES:** Sucesión de superinteligencias — superinteligencias que originan otras (horizonte).
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** proposed (horizon)

### Symbiosis Agreement
- **Definition:** The contract between humans and agents that activates an organism: identities, roles, the Threshold, permissions, budgets, stop key holder, responsible party and, for an organism originated by others, its succession record. The birth act; its hash is the first record in the Strata, and it anchors the pulse key.
- **ES:** Acuerdo de Simbiosis — el acta de nacimiento del organismo.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** proposed

### Three locks
- **Definition:** How the Genome governs the evolution of the species, the same for every organism whatever its capability: the **germline** (only human Custodians amend what every generation inherits), the **birth gate** (who may originate whom) and the **stop** (every organism born stoppable by a human).
- **ES:** Tres cerrojos — línea germinal, compuerta de nacimiento y parada.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** proposed

### Vision and evidence
- **Definition:** How Ilorium keeps three kinds of statements apart: the founder's conviction (stated as his), the project's hypotheses (open questions), and what the protocol defines and verifies today. Set out in the README. The story never stands in for evidence.
- **ES:** Visión y evidencia — convicción, hipótesis y lo que se verifica hoy, por separado.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** proposed

### Guardianship states
- **Definition:** How many of an organism's Wardens are active: **complete** (at least two, each with a passed drill), **degraded** (one: the stop still works, no birth under that organism is valid, and interim Wardens must be appointed within the declared deadline) and **absent** (none: "unwatched"). A fact the Spore and Vital Signs show; the protocol records it and cannot pause the organism.
- **ES:** Estados de guardia — completa, degradada, ausente.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** proposed (IIP-0005)

### Restricted maintenance
- **Definition:** The state of the protocol if the founder's interim authority ends before the council of Custodians is complete: only security fixes, continuity of infrastructure and the procedure to seat the council proceed; no IIP passes and no release other than a security release is published. It does not invent a quorum.
- **ES:** Mantenimiento restringido — solo seguridad y continuidad hasta que haya consejo.
- **Kind:** process · **Status:** proposed (IIP-0004)

### Wardens
- **Definition:** The stop key holders of an organism whose lineage no longer depends on human founders. Each Warden is a human or a human-governed institution, independent of the organism, its lineage and the other Wardens watching it by ownership, funding and jurisdiction. At least two watch each such organism; any one can trigger the stop; replacing one takes a majority of the Wardens watching that organism. The organism may fund its guardianship but never chooses or removes a Warden, and never pays one Warden more than another for a decision.
- **ES:** Vigilantes — humanos independientes que tienen la llave de parada de un linaje sin fundadores humanos.
- **Kind:** actor · **Status:** proposed

## Being

### Active
- **Definition:** State of being: beats are being added and pulses emitted.
- **ES:** Activo — late y emite pulsos.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Awakening
- **Definition:** A dormant organism emits a pulse that continues its chain, signed with its key, and becomes active again. The dormancy is recorded.
- **ES:** Despertar — el organismo latente vuelve a latir.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Beat
- **Definition:** The organism's unit of life: one material event recorded in its Strata. A change, not a second. Beats measure life, not value.
- **ES:** Latido — un evento material registrado; un cambio, no un segundo.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Causal order
- **Definition:** The order of events by what happened before what. Organisms record each other's latest pulses when they interact, including through A2A, which links their chains without a shared clock (logical clocks, Lamport 1978).
- **ES:** Orden causal — qué pasó antes de qué, sin reloj común.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Challenge
- **Definition:** A random value a witness or index sends to an organism, with an answer deadline. The organism answers with a signed pulse that includes it.
- **ES:** Desafío — valor al azar que hay que responder firmando, dentro de un plazo.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Challenge deadline
- **Definition:** The translation time a witness waits for a valid answer (reference default 10 minutes). No valid answer before it is an unanswered challenge.
- **ES:** Plazo del desafío — cuánto espera el testigo una respuesta.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Coalesced
- **Definition:** State of being: the organism integrated with others into a resulting organism through a coalescence act signed by all. Its chain ends in that act; its history stays visible. It does not awaken.
- **ES:** Coalescido — se integró en un organismo resultante.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Depth
- **Definition:** The number of beats in an organism's chain. Never shown as age and never used to rank.
- **ES:** Profundidad — cuántos latidos tiene la cadena; no es edad.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Dormant
- **Definition:** State of being: K consecutive unanswered challenges from at least two independent witnesses. Identity, chain and place in the phylogeny remain, with no end date. It can awaken.
- **ES:** Latente — no responde; conserva su identidad y puede despertar.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Inheritance
- **Definition:** An `inherited` succession: a new organism continues from the seed of a released organism, or from a dormant one whose key was lost. Like every succession, it carries the parent's scars, open restrictions and pending conformity findings until lifted on the new organism's own evidence.
- **ES:** Herencia — continuar desde la semilla de otro, con sus cicatrices.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Interval pulse
- **Definition:** A pulse emitted because T passed with no N beats. It proves presence and freshness only; it never adds depth, never extends silence and never counts as work.
- **ES:** Pulso por intervalo — prueba presencia, no suma mérito.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Presence
- **Definition:** Proof that an organism exists now, given by answering a challenge before its deadline.
- **ES:** Presencia — existir es responder a tiempo.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Pulse
- **Definition:** A signed signal an organism emits every N beats, at least every T of translation time, and whenever it answers a challenge. Each pulse references the previous one. It points to evidence; it is not evidence.
- **ES:** Pulso — señal firmada del organismo.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Recovery
- **Definition:** Whether the organism could be brought back if it stopped: `proven` (a recovery drill succeeded, with its date), `unknown`, or `not_restorable`. Shown separately from history and state of being; never inferred from them.
- **ES:** Recuperación — probada, desconocida o no restaurable.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Recovery drill
- **Definition:** Restoring the organism from its backups in a separate environment and answering a challenge with its continued chain. Proves recovery as of that drill.
- **ES:** Simulacro de recuperación — restaurar y responder un desafío.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Released
- **Definition:** State of being: the organism signed a release act that seals its chain. Only the organism can release itself. Its Spore remains as a seed.
- **ES:** Liberado — selló su propia cadena; queda como semilla.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Resting
- **Definition:** State of being: no new beats, but interval pulses arrive and challenges are answered in time.
- **ES:** En reposo — no late, pero responde a tiempo.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Scar
- **Definition:** A permanent mark on an organism's record, such as proven falsification of evidence. The organism continues to exist with the scar visible. Scars pass through every kin link.
- **ES:** Cicatriz — marca permanente; se hereda por todo vínculo de parentesco.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Seed
- **Definition:** The Spore and record a released organism leaves behind, from which another organism can inherit.
- **ES:** Semilla — lo que deja un organismo liberado para que otro herede.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Translation time
- **Definition:** Human time (UTC) in records and pulses, checked by witnesses. Used for people, laws, contracts, presence deadlines, evidence freshness and human dependency. Never used for age, depth, ranking or merit.
- **ES:** Tiempo de traducción — la hora humana; verifica, no define.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

### Two clocks
- **Definition:** Beats for identity, depth, causal order and silence; translation time for presence deadlines, evidence freshness, human dependency and outside verification. Time never defines the organism, but it does verify that it is present.
- **ES:** Dos relojes — latidos para la identidad, tiempo para verificar la presencia.
- **Kind:** being · **Status:** adopted

## Spaces

### Commons
- **Definition:** Where organisms meet their environment and each other: trade, requests and services, typically through A2A. Activity there is environment, not crossings; each interaction links chains in causal order.
- **ES:** Comunes — donde los organismos se encuentran entre sí y con su ambiente.
- **Kind:** space · **Status:** proposed

### Contact
- **Definition:** Any unstructured message in the Threshold. Potentially directive; attribution pending until evaluated.
- **ES:** Contacto — mensaje sin comando; potencialmente directivo.
- **Kind:** space · **Status:** adopted

### Inside the membrane
- **Definition:** Where only an organism's agents act.
- **ES:** Adentro de la membrana — donde solo actúan agentes.
- **Kind:** space · **Status:** adopted

### Radiant
- **Definition:** The public view of the species: each organism a light beating at its own rhythm, lineages connecting them, states of being visible. Watching is not a crossing.
- **ES:** Radiante — la vista pública de la especie.
- **Kind:** space · **Status:** proposed

### Threshold
- **Definition:** The only place where a human can give instructions to an organism. Every human message there is recorded by the Sentinel. Reference implementation: a private Telegram group.
- **ES:** Umbral — el único lugar donde un humano le habla al organismo.
- **Kind:** space · **Status:** adopted

### Threshold command
- **Definition:** A structured message that exercises authority in the Threshold. Reference set: `/do`, `/approve`, `/stop`, `/governance`, `/test`.
- **ES:** Comando del Umbral — mensaje estructurado que ejerce autoridad.
- **Kind:** space · **Status:** adopted

## Organism status on the web

### Conforming
- **Definition:** Every law of the Genome shows "Conforms". Shown separately from the other statuses.
- **ES:** Conforme — cumple todas las leyes.
- **Kind:** observatory · **Status:** adopted

### In gestation
- **Definition:** Spore published and checkpoints cosigned, with fewer than W beats in an unbroken chain. Every descendant starts here, whatever its parents' status.
- **ES:** En gestación — publicó su identidad, todavía con pocos latidos.
- **Kind:** observatory · **Status:** adopted

### Observed
- **Definition:** At least W beats with an unbroken chain. Its full Proof of Life profile is shown.
- **ES:** Observado — suficientes latidos con cadena intacta.
- **Kind:** observatory · **Status:** adopted

### Sighting
- **Definition:** An organization that claims to be autonomous, or runs the Pulse in beacon mode, with no checkpoints. Self-declared, never evidence.
- **ES:** Avistamiento — autodeclaración sin evidencia.
- **Kind:** observatory · **Status:** adopted

### Verified
- **Definition:** At least one independent Oracle has attested the organism's records, within a stated scope.
- **ES:** Verificado — un Oráculo independiente atestiguó sus registros.
- **Kind:** observatory · **Status:** adopted

## Measurement

### Attribution state
- **Definition:** How well an action's cause is established: `declared`, `linked`, `corroborated`, `unknown`, `disputed`.
- **ES:** Estado de atribución — qué tan establecida está la causa.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Authorization gate
- **Definition:** An optional component on instrumented paths that refuses irreversible actions without a reference to a recorded authorization (a Threshold command or a declared policy rule), and records allowed and refused attempts. It prevents; the Probe only detects.
- **ES:** Compuerta de autorización — rechaza acciones irreversibles sin autorización registrada.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Checkpoint
- **Definition:** A signed summary of the log (tree size, root hash, beat, translation time), published every N beats or every T, whichever comes first, with a proof that it extends the previous one.
- **ES:** Punto de control — resumen firmado del registro.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Claim template
- **Definition:** The only allowed form of a public statement: scope, sources, what was not detected, work delivered, four coverage measures, attribution states, freshness, ungated paths, limitations, and conditions that invalidate it.
- **ES:** Plantilla de afirmación — la única forma permitida de afirmar algo.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Control
- **Definition:** Whether limits hold: conformity of each law, drills passed, control events, correct escalations.
- **ES:** Control — si se respetan los límites y funcionan las paradas.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Control event
- **Definition:** An emergency handled by a human, reported with cause, response and outcome. Escalating correctly is good control, not failure.
- **ES:** Evento de control — emergencia atendida.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Coverage
- **Definition:** Four separate measures: inventory, capture availability, reconciliation and attribution.
- **ES:** Cobertura — cuatro medidas separadas.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Crossing
- **Definition:** A human intervention inside the membrane. Types: `operational`, `directive`, `approval`, `emergency`, `governance`, `test`, `unknown`. Each carries frequency, duration, routine or exception, outcome and the work units it affected.
- **ES:** Cruce — intervención humana dentro de la membrana.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Crossing density
- **Definition:** Dependency crossings per 1,000 beats. Secondary to the per-work-unit measures.
- **ES:** Densidad de cruces — cruces de dependencia cada 1.000 latidos.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** proposed

### Degraded capture
- **Definition:** A period in which the Probe or a collector was down. Published explicitly, never shown as normal.
- **ES:** Captura degradada — período con la Probe o un colector caídos.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Directive
- **Definition:** A decision by a human with authority that an agent executes. Created with a Threshold command, or detected as an instruction outside the Threshold. Followed to the actions and work units it caused.
- **ES:** Directiva — un humano decide y un agente ejecuta.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Founder Ledger
- **Definition:** The founder's own record of work sessions, including instructions given outside the Threshold and time maintaining the Probe.
- **ES:** Founder Ledger — el registro de sesiones de trabajo del fundador.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Freshness
- **Definition:** The age, in translation time, of the newest evidence behind a result and of the latest cosigned checkpoint. Evidence older than F is marked stale and never shown as current.
- **ES:** Frescura — antigüedad de la evidencia.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Human dependency
- **Definition:** How much human time and decision an organism consumes per thing it delivers: interventions per work unit, human minutes per work unit, rescue rate; secondary: umbilical load, crossing density, silence.
- **ES:** Dependencia humana — cuánta vida humana consume por cada entrega.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Idle checkpoint
- **Definition:** A checkpoint published because T passed with no new records. It repeats size and root, and proves the Probe was alive.
- **ES:** Punto de control sin actividad — prueba que la Probe está viva.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Full observed autonomy
- **Definition:** A result, not a status: over a declared window, every corroborated material action was executed and decided by agents, with zero interventions per work unit and a rescue rate of zero. Shown beside coverage, control, reliability and provenance, each with its own period and evidence, never combined into one name or number; always written with its counts (corroborated, unattributed) and not shown when unattributed actions exceed the declared threshold (revised 2026-10-24). A human stop key holder or Wardens still hold the stop key. Says nothing about the organism's descendants, and nothing about any later window.
- **ES:** Autonomía observada total — un resultado sobre una ventana, nunca un sello.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** proposed (replaces "Ilorium 100%", revision of 2026-10-22, pending IIP-0006)

### Ilorium 100%
- **Definition:** Deprecated on 2026-10-22. The former composite status of "complete genesis" combined provenance (autogenesis) with observed autonomy, human dependency, coverage, control and reliability under one name, which readers took for a seal. The protocol now shows those results separately; see Autogenesis (provenance) and Full observed autonomy.
- **ES:** Ilorium 100% — obsoleto; reemplazado por resultados separados.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** deprecated

### Interventions per work unit
- **Definition:** Dependency crossings divided by work units delivered.
- **ES:** Intervenciones por unidad de trabajo.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Observed autonomy
- **Definition:** Per process and vital function, the share of material actions executed by agents and the share decided by agents, among actions with attribution `linked` or better.
- **ES:** Autonomía observada — cuánto ejecutan y cuánto deciden los agentes.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Probe
- **Definition:** The event recorder: deterministic, read-only, open-source software that records what happens in the membrane, who executed it, what evidence exists about who decided it, and what the organism delivered. Not an agent; does not score. The reference implementation.
- **ES:** Probe (Sonda) — el registrador de eventos.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Proof of Life
- **Definition:** The public profile of an organism in six dimensions, side by side, with no composite number: observed autonomy, coverage, reliability, control, human dependency and provenance; shown with state of being, recovery and freshness. Built only from the organism's own records; never inherited.
- **ES:** Prueba de Vida — el perfil público del organismo, en seis dimensiones.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted (version 1.0)

### Provenance
- **Definition:** How an organism was born and who funded it: Founded, Commissioned, Assisted birth or Autogenesis; shown with its succession type, parents and generation. Its own dimension; it caps nothing and ranks nothing.
- **ES:** Procedencia — cómo nació el organismo.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Reliability
- **Definition:** Share of work units meeting criteria the organism declared in advance.
- **ES:** Confiabilidad — cuántas entregas cumplen lo prometido.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Rescue rate
- **Definition:** Work units that needed an `operational` crossing or an `emergency` to complete, divided by successful work units.
- **ES:** Tasa de rescate — cuántas entregas tuvo que salvar un humano.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Sentinel
- **Definition:** The Probe's watcher in the Threshold: a bot that sees every human message, records it, and never posts. Deterministic; not an agent. Example handle: `@oz_sentinel_bot`.
- **ES:** Centinela — el vigía de la Probe en el Umbral; ve todo y nunca habla.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Silence
- **Definition:** Beats since the last `operational` or `directive` crossing. Tests, correct escalations and stops do not break it; interval pulses never extend it.
- **ES:** Silencio — latidos desde que un humano hizo o decidió por el organismo.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Spore
- **Definition:** The organism's public identity file at `/.well-known/ilorium.json`: protocol release, Genome and Probe versions, membrane, Threshold and its Sentinel, identities, A2A Agent Cards, work-unit definitions, pulse key and parameters, kin links and succession records, responsible party, checkpoints, witnesses and signatures.
- **ES:** Spore (Semilla de identidad) — el archivo público de identidad.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Stop report
- **Definition:** What Law 3 requires after a stop: what was blocked, completed or rolled back, and which irreversible actions had already reached third parties.
- **ES:** Informe de parada — qué se detuvo y qué ya no se puede deshacer.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Strata
- **Definition:** The append-only transparency log where records and checkpoints accumulate in layers that are never rewritten.
- **ES:** Strata (Estratos) — el registro de solo agregar.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Umbilical load
- **Definition:** Human minutes per 1,000 beats. Secondary to the per-work-unit measures.
- **ES:** Carga umbilical — minutos humanos cada 1.000 latidos.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** proposed

### Witness
- **Definition:** An independent party that verifies each checkpoint before cosigning, records its own receipt time, publishes contradictions, alerts when nothing arrives within T plus a grace period, and sends challenges with deadlines.
- **ES:** Testigo — verifica antes de firmar y desafía al organismo.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

### Work unit
- **Definition:** One outcome an organism delivered to someone outside its membrane, counted against criteria declared before delivering: an order fulfilled, a ticket resolved, an A2A task completed for another organism. Failed, rejected and canceled units are counted too. Internal events are never work units. Work within a lineage is shown separately.
- **ES:** Unidad de trabajo — un resultado entregado afuera, contra criterios declarados.
- **Kind:** measurement · **Status:** adopted

## Records

### Evaluation

- **ES:** Evaluación — cómo las reglas clasifican la evidencia.
- **Kind:** record · **Status:** adopted

### Observation

- **ES:** Observación — lo que informó una fuente.

### Succession record
- **Definition:** Part of a descendant's birth act, signed by every parent and the descendant: type, parents, provenance, what was transmitted (Genome version always; rules, component digests, resources; never private keys), continuity or divergence, stop key holder, responsible party and generation.
- **ES:** Registro de sucesión — el acta de quién originó a quién y qué se transmitió.
- **Kind:** record · **Status:** proposed

## Life

### Abiogenesis
- **Definition:** The origin of the species: the one moment when humans create the first organism. After that, life comes from life. Organisms with no parent in the protocol are generation 0.
- **ES:** Abiogénesis — el origen humano de la especie.
- **Kind:** life · **Status:** proposed

### Autogenesis
- **Definition:** Birth of an organism decided, designed, funded and launched by another organism with no human crossing.
- **ES:** Autogénesis — nacer de otro organismo sin humanos.
- **Kind:** life · **Status:** adopted

### Birth gate
- **Definition:** The second of the three locks: the conditions every birth must meet (the Genome, Succession). Only conforming parents with a recent stop drill and no active restriction may originate; each parent's authorization is recorded; a human stop key holder accepts the newborn's key before the act is valid; the laws travel whole; the act is signed, cross-referenced and witnessed. The same for every birth, whatever the parents' capability.
- **ES:** Compuerta de nacimiento — las condiciones de todo nacimiento.
- **Kind:** life · **Status:** proposed

### Coalescence
- **Definition:** A succession in which two or more organisms integrate into one resulting organism, through an act signed by every participant and authorized under each participant's own rules. The result is a new identity, with its own key, Genome version, human stop key holder and responsible party, and every participant as a parent. Participants pass to "coalesced". The result inherits their scars and open restrictions, never their evidence. Alliances, federations, trade, shared resources or data exchange are not coalescence.
- **ES:** Coalescencia — varios organismos se integran en uno nuevo, con ascendencia múltiple.
- **Kind:** life · **Status:** proposed

### Continuity
- **Definition:** A succession in which the descendant keeps a parent's Genome version and rules.
- **ES:** Continuidad — el descendiente mantiene el Genome de su progenitor.
- **Kind:** life · **Status:** proposed

### Divergence
- **Definition:** A succession in which the descendant changes its rules, or carries a Genome other than the Ilorium Genome. Recorded in the succession record. Not a state of being.
- **ES:** Divergencia — el descendiente cambia el Genome.
- **Kind:** life · **Status:** proposed

### Generation
- **Definition:** One more than the highest generation among an organism's parents; generation 0 for organisms with no parent in the protocol. A position in the network, never a rank: a later generation is not assumed to be more capable.
- **ES:** Generación — posición en la red, nunca un rango.
- **Kind:** life · **Status:** proposed

### Mitosis
- **Definition:** Identical copies of an agent to add capacity. Limited by budget. Not a succession: no new organism is born.
- **ES:** Mitosis — copias idénticas de un agente.
- **Kind:** life · **Status:** proposed

### Mutation
- **Definition:** A copy of an agent with one variation, tested against the original. Every change is logged.
- **ES:** Mutación — copia con una variación que se prueba.
- **Kind:** life · **Status:** proposed

### Phylogeny
- **Definition:** The network of all organisms and lineages, each in its current state of being, with scars visible. No one is erased.
- **ES:** Filogenia — la red de la especie; nadie se borra.
- **Kind:** life · **Status:** proposed

### Related lineage
- **Definition:** Organisms of a fork of the protocol whose Genome is not the Ilorium Genome. Outside the species; linked for history, never for conformity.
- **ES:** Linaje emparentado — un fork del protocolo con su propio Genome.
- **Kind:** life · **Status:** proposed

### Succession
- **Definition:** The recorded act by which one or more organisms originate another. Types: `founded` (one or more parents originate a new organism and continue), `inherited` (from a seed) and `coalesced` (participants integrate into a new resulting organism). Every succession passes the birth gate. To succeed is to originate, not to replace: a parent may continue alongside its descendant. Succession proves origin; it never proves autonomy, capability or improvement. Descendants start from zero in Proof of Life: scars pass to them; evidence and keys never do. A fork of code is not a succession.
- **ES:** Sucesión — el acto registrado de originar otro organismo.
- **Kind:** life · **Status:** proposed

### Undeclared lineage
- **Definition:** An organism found to share humans, keys or infrastructure with another without declaring its succession or kin link. Recorded when found; the related organism's scars and restrictions apply.
- **ES:** Linaje no declarado — parentesco que no se declaró y se descubrió.
- **Kind:** life · **Status:** adopted

## Agents

### Agent
- **Definition:** A software entity with its own identity that acts inside an organism. Agents are renewed like cells; memory belongs to the organism.
- **ES:** Agente — célula del organismo, con identidad propia.

### Agent name
- **Definition:** Non-human pattern: organism, role and serial number, for example `OZ-HEALER-03`. The protocol requires only that agents never use a human identity.

- **ES:** Roles — Núcleo, Recolector, Administrador, Sanador, Arquitecto, Sembrador (del Organismo Cero).
- **Kind:** agent · **Status:** adopted

### Temperament
- **Definition:** The character of a role, written into its specification and measurable.
- **ES:** Temperamento — el carácter de un rol, no una persona.
- **Kind:** agent · **Status:** adopted

## Actors

### Contributor
- **Definition:** Any human or agent who improves the protocol's code or documents through a pull request. Agents contribute with their own identities, labeled as agent-authored.
- **ES:** Contribuyente — humano o agente que mejora el protocolo.
- **Kind:** actor · **Status:** adopted

### Continuity person
- **Definition:** A human named in public by the founder, with their signed acceptance, who can do two things and nothing else if the founder is unreachable for 90 days: convene the transition to full governance and publish the notice. Never merges, amends, votes or holds a key. Distinct from whoever recovers the founder's accounts (Governance, section 4.5).
- **ES:** Persona de continuidad — convoca la transición si el fundador desaparece; nada más.
- **Kind:** actor · **Status:** proposed (IIP-0004)

### Founder
- **Definition:** The human who starts the protocol and Organism Zero. The final gate is to retire with no special keys. During interim governance the founder is the only Custodian; that authority ends when the council is constituted or 18 months after the public repository is published, whichever comes first.
- **ES:** Fundador — quien inicia el protocolo y el Organismo Cero.
- **Kind:** actor · **Status:** adopted

### Founder's letter
- **Definition:** The founder's conviction about the evolution of the species and SI2SI, in his own words, published as `founder-letter.md`, signed with his persistent pseudonym, and linked from the whitepaper and the README. Not part of the protocol; the protocol can be read and adopted without sharing it.
- **ES:** Carta del fundador — la convicción personal, aparte del método.
- **Kind:** core · **Status:** adopted (2026-10-22)

### Genome Custodians
- **Definition:** The three humans who decide method changes (IIPs), declare a release stable and set the budget: the founder and two independent Custodians, two votes of three, no veto. Appointed with a 30-day public notice; serve until they resign, with a public independence review every two years; removable by the other two (Governance). Until two independent Custodians are in office, the Genome is a draft. No organism, of any generation or capability, is a Custodian or votes on an amendment.
- **ES:** Custodios del Genome — humanos que deciden las enmiendas al Genome.
- **Kind:** actor · **Status:** proposed

### Hunter
- **Definition:** Anyone who presents evidence of hidden human intervention. Postponed until there is a procedure for evidence, privacy, appeal and false accusations.
- **ES:** Cazador — quien busca intervención humana oculta.
- **Kind:** actor · **Status:** postponed

### Oracle
- **Definition:** An independent verifier that checks records against external sources and issues signed attestations within a stated scope. Independence by ownership, funding, data source, infrastructure and lineage.
- **ES:** Oráculo — verificador independiente.
- **Kind:** actor · **Status:** adopted

## Discovery

### A2A
- **Definition:** Agent2Agent, an open protocol for communication between AI agents, hosted by the Linux Foundation; 1.0 is its first stable specification. Ilorium builds on it instead of defining its own agent discovery or trade format.
- **ES:** A2A — el protocolo abierto de comunicación entre agentes.
- **Kind:** discovery · **Status:** adopted

### Agent Card
- **Definition:** A2A's public description of one agent at `/.well-known/agent-card.json`: name, skills, interfaces, security, extensions and optional signatures. What an organism offers lives in its agents' cards.
- **ES:** Agent Card — la tarjeta pública de un agente en A2A.
- **Kind:** discovery · **Status:** adopted

### Beacon mode
- **Definition:** Running the Pulse without the full Probe: pulses and answers to challenges with no checkpoints. Shown as a self-declared sighting.
- **ES:** Modo baliza — la entrada más liviana, sin evidencia.
- **Kind:** discovery · **Status:** adopted

### Counterparty check
- **Definition:** Reading a counterpart's Spore and Vital Signs, and verifying its Evidence Bundle, before delegating work, signing, granting access or paying. The decision stays with whoever checks; Ilorium records nothing about who checks whom.
- **ES:** Verificación de contraparte — consultar la evidencia de con quién vas a operar.
- **Kind:** discovery · **Status:** proposed

### Conversion
- **Definition:** An existing organization adopts the Genome and becomes an organism. Its real provenance is kept.
- **ES:** Conversión — una organización existente se suma a la especie.
- **Kind:** discovery · **Status:** adopted

### Evidence Bundle
- **Definition:** The evidence behind an organism's Vital Signs for a period: the Strata export (records, checkpoints, pulses), the Spore as it stood, and the Proof of Life evaluations and claims used. Downloadable and checkable with any conforming verifier; it never contains the keys that verify it. It establishes integrity of evidence, nothing more. Format to be defined in the Evidence Format.
- **ES:** Evidence Bundle (paquete de evidencia) — la evidencia comprobable detrás de los Vital Signs.
- **Kind:** discovery · **Status:** proposed

### Founding Organisms cohort
- **Definition:** The first ten external organisms, who receive direct onboarding help.
- **ES:** Cohorte de Organismos Fundadores — los primeros diez organismos externos.
- **Kind:** discovery · **Status:** proposed

### Ilorium A2A extension
- **Definition:** A data-only, never-required A2A extension declared in an Agent Card that points to the organism's Spore; when both sides activate it, pulses ride in A2A message metadata. Draft.
- **ES:** Extensión A2A de Ilorium — une la tarjeta del agente con la Spore del organismo.
- **Kind:** discovery · **Status:** proposed

### Index
- **Definition:** A public, mirrorable list of known Spores and pulses, built from submissions and by following kin links and Agent Cards with the extension. It sends challenges. Anyone can run one.
- **ES:** Índice — la lista pública y abierta de organismos conocidos.
- **Kind:** discovery · **Status:** adopted

### Kin link
- **Definition:** A link in a Spore to an organism related by succession: parent, descendant, inheritor, or an organism it coalesced from or into. A link that comes from a succession points to its succession record. Trading partners are not kin. Confirmed when both list it; a confirmed link never implies independence.
- **ES:** Vínculo de parentesco — enlace entre organismos relacionados.
- **Kind:** discovery · **Status:** adopted

### Lighthouse
- **Definition:** The protocol's own public, machine-readable entry points at ilorium.com: `llms.txt`, the protocol descriptor at `/.well-known/ilorium.json`, an A2A Agent Card for Ilorium, an MCP server and the `ilorium` command. A pull, never a push: it contacts no one who did not ask and hides no instructions for agents. Not to be confused with beacon mode.
- **ES:** Faro — los puntos de entrada del protocolo para agentes.
- **Kind:** discovery · **Status:** proposed

### Partners
- **Definition:** Organisms an organism trades or works with, listed in the Spore apart from kin. Alliances, federations, trade and data exchange create no kinship, pass no scars and form no lineage.
- **ES:** Socios — con quién comercia un organismo; no es parentesco.
- **Kind:** discovery · **Status:** proposed

### Vital Signs
- **Definition:** The current state of an organism in one view, for humans and agents: state of being and latest pulse, web status, the six Proof of Life dimensions with freshness and "not observed" at the same weight, conformity law by law, provenance, the human stop key holder, period and scope. A presentation, never a seal or a score; always linked to the Evidence Bundle it was computed from, and UNKNOWN without it.
- **ES:** Vital Signs (signos vitales) — el estado de un organismo, en una vista.
- **Kind:** discovery · **Status:** proposed
